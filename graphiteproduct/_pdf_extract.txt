C/C composite — heat treatment racks and shelves
Lightweight C/C racks with near-zero thermal expansion.
• High-density C/C racks assembled by machining and splicing
• CTE ~0.5×10⁻⁶/°C vs. stainless steel 10–17×10⁻⁶/°C
• Service up to 3200 °C in oxygen-free environment
• Density ~1.5 g/cm³: lightweight, easy stacking and assembly
• Carbon content ≥99.9%: chemically inert, no reaction with metal workpieces

---PAGE---

C/C composite — heat-treated carrier plate
Crack-resistant C/C carrier plates for demanding heat treatment.
• Lower density (1.5 g/cm³) than mullite/SiC; higher strength than graphite
• Compressive strength ≥180 MPa; flexural strength ≥120 MPa
• High toughness: no cracking under impact
• Better oxidation resistance and longer life than graphite
• Graphitization-related lubricity protects workpiece surfaces
• Suitable for diverse heat treatment environments up to 3200 °C (inert)
